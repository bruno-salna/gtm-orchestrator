# Examples

**Everything in this folder is made up.** "Examplia" (`XX`) is a fictional country, and every company, number and date is invented. The files show the shape of a run, not real data.

The setting is the one the other four guides' examples use: a company sells production-planning software to food producers, has 39 customers, and two people who can each work about 40 new accounts a month. Here the user arrives with nothing, so the run starts at the Market map and goes through all four guides.

- [run-brief.md](run-brief.md): the brief from step 2, written before any data was shown
- [run-log.md](run-log.md): every guide run, gate, loop-back and decision, including the four gates in full
- [run-summary.md](run-summary.md): the one-page summary from step 7

The run shows the common cases: one loop-back at gate 1 (the first market was smaller than tier 1, so the user widened it), a hard limit kept out of the map and applied in tiering, a map checked with the market sizing preset so the ICP compares against the whole market, gates that pass, a gate that passes with notes, and labels and gaps carried into the summary.

The ICP + tiering and Signals numbers are the ones in those guides' own examples. The market here is wider than the one in the Market map guide's example, because of the loop-back at gate 1.
